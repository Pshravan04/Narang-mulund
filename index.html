Narang Mulund, Sarvodaya Nagar – Elevated Luxury Living

Narang Mulund is an upcoming 5-acre luxury residential development by Narang Developers, nestled in the peaceful and coveted address of Sarvodaya Nagar, Mulund West. Featuring 2 premium high-rise towers standing tall at Ground + 41 Storeys, this project is crafted for those seeking expansive living, supreme privacy, and architectural grandeur in one of Mumbai's most established residential suburbs.

Each tower is curated with only 6 apartments per floor, creating an ultra-private community of approximately 200 select residences. Homeowners can choose between sprawling 3 BHK residences (1,200+ sq. ft.) and grand 4 BHK residences (2,000+ sq. ft.), each engineered for optimum natural daylight, cross-ventilation, and majestic views. With pre-launch pricing at approximately ₹40,000+ / sq. ft. (starting from ₹4.80 Cr*), Narang Mulund offers an unmatched opportunity to invest in a premier lifestyle address with superb connectivity via GMLR, Eastern Express Highway, and upcoming Metro lines. About Narang Developers

Narang Developers is a distinguished real estate development brand recognized for creating high-quality, architecturally refined residential and commercial landmarks across Mumbai. With a focus on innovative engineering, aesthetic finesse, and customer-first values, the group has earned the trust of hundreds of families.

Narang Mulund is their premier upcoming luxury residential project located at Sarvodaya Nagar, Mulund West. Spread over 5 expansive acres featuring two 41-storey towers and only 6 residences per floor, this boutique development reflects a philosophy of low-density luxury, supreme privacy, and enduring value.
